  summary: Kim builds animal forms such as deer, horses and birds by joining vivid coloured pieces onto stainless-steel sculpture. More than 40 solo exhibitions and many art fairs and group shows in Korea and abroad. His works are held at over 70 sites, including housing complexes, hotels and corporate buildings.
  note: Kim's animal sculptures read as one silhouette from afar and as a pattern of countless colour pieces up close. Their forms are recognised by children and adults alike, so in busy lobbies, complex entrances and plazas they become places where people stop and take photos. They are already installed at more than 70 housing complexes, commercial facilities and hotels.
