  name: Kim Byoungho
  medium: Aluminium, stainless steel, brass
  born: '1974'
  based: Seoul
  summary: Trained in printmaking and engineering, Kim builds his sculptures from precise technical drawings and a divided production system instead of hand drawing, in metal works where one unit repeats and multiplies. More than 15 solo exhibitions and over 100 group exhibitions in Korea and abroad.
  hero_caption: 236 Silent Propagations, 2022
  note: In Kim's sculpture a single unit repeats tens or hundreds of times until it becomes a garden. Each work starts as a drawing and is finished in the factory, so it keeps its precision at any scale. It anchors high lobbies and atriums and the public space in front of a building. He has installed works in hotels, department stores, housing complexes and corporate headquarters.
